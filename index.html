Important Notes & Motivation

  • Start with Free Software: DaVinci Resolve is an incredible tool and you do not need to pay for an expensive program to become a pro.
  • Practice, Practice, Practice: Your editing muscle will only get stronger with every project you finish.
  • Learn from Others: Don't try to learn everything on your own. There are thousands of amazing, free tutorials on YouTube.
  • You are a storyteller: A good editor is a good storyteller. Your job is to make your audience feel something.
